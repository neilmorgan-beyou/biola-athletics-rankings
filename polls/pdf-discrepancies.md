# NAIA week-by-week PDF vs. poll archive: discrepancies

Generated 2026-09-30 while filling missing NAIA polls from the NAIA week-by-week PDFs. Nothing listed here was changed in the archive; existing polls were left exactly as they were.

How to read it: each entry is an archived poll whose date matches a PDF column (within 3 days), but where the PDF ranks a team differently. Name-only differences were ignored, for example 'LSU-Shreveport' vs 'Louisiana State University-Shreveport' or 'Mountain State' vs 'College of West Virginia'. 'PDF' is what NAIA's week-by-week PDF prints. 'Archive' is what the archived poll has at that rank, plus the rank the archive gives that same team. 'none' means the archive has no team at that rank, usually because of a tie.
