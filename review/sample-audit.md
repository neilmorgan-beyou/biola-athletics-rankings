# Rankings history: adversarial sample audit

Audited 2026-09-29. Source file: `rankings-merged.json` (`rows`, 1,248 rows). The sample was drawn with `random.seed(7)`: up to 3 rows per sport (preferring 2 secondary/biola-news rows each), plus 8 more `secondary` rows and 12 more rows ranked 1-5. That gives **73 rows**: 33 biola-news, 26 primary and 14 secondary, 28 of them ranked 1-5. Every source_url was fetched. Sites that blocked the fetch (403/404) were retried through the Wayback Machine.

Verdict key: **OK** = Biola at that rank, poll, sport and season in the source. **OK\*** = rank confirmed, but the date or poll name is inferred, or the source is internally inconsistent. **MISMATCH** = the row is wrong or not supported by its source. **SOURCE DEAD** = the source cannot be reached. **CAN'T TELL** = the source is too weak or ambiguous to confirm.
